# Hi, I'm Claudio 👋

I'm a **front-end developer and digital designer** from the Netherlands. I enjoy combining design and technology to create digital experiences that are intuitive, accessible and visually engaging.

I studied **Communication and Multimedia Design** at the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences, where I developed a strong foundation in UX design, visual design and front-end development.

- 💻 Building with React, Next.js and TypeScript
- 🎨 Designing interfaces and prototypes in Figma
- 🧠 Interested in UX, interaction design and creative development
- 🌱 Always learning and experimenting with new tools
- 🇳🇱 Based in the Netherlands
